I built this site for the audience I most respect: in-house performance marketers running their own programs without an agency layer. They make better decisions when they have data, and most published “best PPC tools” reviews don’t give them any. The 90-day benchmark on the homepage is my attempt to fix that.

Why a benchmark, not a ranking

Rankings imply the writer knows your situation. They don’t. A benchmark gives you the numbers and lets you decide which one matches your account. The site provides both: ranked recommendations for the typical case, and the underlying benchmark so you can override based on your context.

How tools earn a spot

The same fixed framework I use for agency standardization decisions: three accounts, 90-day window, revenue-weighted ROAS as primary metric, anonymized reporting. Tools that don’t deliver statistically meaningful lift on at least two of three test accounts don’t make the list, regardless of vendor relationships.

What I won’t do

Sponsored placements, affiliate-for-ranking arrangements, paid review tiers — all declined.
