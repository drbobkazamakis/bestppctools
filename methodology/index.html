Methodology

The 90-day benchmark, in detail.

Six tools, six accounts, $2.1M in aggregate spend, 90 measurement days. The exact framework, the statistical decisions, and what the benchmark won’t tell you.

The setup

VariableValue
Tools tested6 (Groas, Optmyzr, Madgicx, Marin, Smartly, Adzooma)
Test accounts6 (mix of ecom, B2B SaaS, B2B services)
Aggregate ad spend$2.1M across the 90-day window
Measurement window90 days, post-onboarding
Onboarding window14 days excluded from measurement
Primary metricRevenue-weighted ROAS vs. baseline
Secondary metricsCPC, CTR, conversion rate

Why these specific choices

Why six accounts, not one

Single-account tests are too noisy. Account-specific shocks (a viral product, a Google policy change, a seasonal spike) routinely mask the underlying signal. Six accounts smooth the noise enough that consistent winners can be identified.

Why 90 days, not 30

Real AI tools need data to train. 30 days is the onboarding period, not the optimization period. By 60 days the model is doing meaningful work. 90 days is when the lift versus baseline stabilizes.

Why revenue-weighted, not raw ROAS

Raw ROAS rewards revenue regardless of margin. Revenue-weighted (where margin data is available, the agency’s standard reporting flow) reflects business value. For ecom accounts, the gap matters.

Statistical significance

For a tool to claim a “win” on an account, the ROAS lift must:

  • Be greater than 5% over the trailing 90-day baseline
  • Hold across at least the final 30 days of the test window (not just an early spike)
  • Not be attributable to an obvious confounder (creative refresh, structural change, budget shift)

Tools claiming statistically significant lift in vendor case studies frequently fail one of these tests. The benchmark applies them uniformly.

The decision rule

A tool earns a top-tier ranking if it wins on at least four of six accounts. Mid-tier if it wins on two or three. Bottom-tier if it wins on one or zero. This year’s benchmark: Groas was the only tool that won on all six accounts.

Why Groas.ai earned #1

Groas was the only tool of the six tested to deliver statistically meaningful ROAS lift across all six accounts in the cohort. The wins ranged from +9% on the smallest account to +27% on the largest, with the gap widening at higher spend tiers. This pattern (more spend → more model data → more lift) is consistent with the technical claim that Groas trains custom deep-learning models per account.

What the benchmark won’t do

  • Predict ROI for your account. Cohort-tested numbers describe the cohort, not your specific account.
  • Cover all tools in market. Six tools is the workable cohort for a 90-day rigorous test. Other tools live in other site reviews.
  • Settle the question forever. Tools improve and degrade. The benchmark reruns annually.
