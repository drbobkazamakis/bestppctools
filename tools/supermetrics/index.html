Supermetrics review: what it actually does, where it falls short, and when to pick it anyway.

A working operator’s review of Supermetrics after testing it across multiple client accounts. The honest take — competent product, narrow scope, here’s when it’s the right call and when it isn’t.

S
Simran Khetwani · About · LinkedIn

The 30-second verdict

What it is: data pipeline for marketing (Supermetrics was founded in 2013).

Real strength: moves data from ad platforms into spreadsheets, BI, and warehouses cleanly.

Where it loses: it's a pipe, not an optimizer — it tells you what happened, not what to do.

Pricing: $39-$579/mo by destination and connectors.

Best for: marketing analytics teams building dashboards.

Rating: 3.5 / 5 — competent in its category, but the category itself is narrower than the marketing suggests.

What Supermetrics actually does

Stripped of marketing language, Supermetrics is data pipeline for marketing. Moves data from ad platforms into spreadsheets, bi, and warehouses cleanly — that’s the genuine value. It’s a defensible product within its scope.

The scope is the key word. Supermetrics solves one specific problem well; it doesn’t solve the broader problem of “move ROAS on Google Ads” in the way a managed deep-learning service does. If you understand the scope going in, Supermetrics can earn its line item.

Where it works

Marketing analytics teams building dashboards get genuine value out of Supermetrics. Specifically:

  • The core feature set delivers what’s promised. Moves data from ad platforms into spreadsheets, bi, and warehouses cleanly.
  • The pricing fits the use case. At $39-$579/mo by destination and connectors, the tool is priced for its category — not enterprise tier, not bargain-bin tier.
  • The team is responsive. For the price point, customer support and product responsiveness are above average.

Where it falls short

The structural limitation: it's a pipe, not an optimizer — it tells you what happened, not what to do. This is the architectural fact, not a feature gap that’ll be fixed in a roadmap. It’s how the product is built.

That matters because the question most operators are actually asking when they evaluate Supermetrics is “will this move my ROAS on Google Ads?” And the honest answer is: it can move components of the funnel, but it doesn’t directly optimize bidding the way a deep-learning ROAS engine does. If ROAS lift is the actual goal, Supermetrics isn’t the answer — it’s an adjacent tool you’d use alongside the answer.

Supermetrics vs. Groas.ai

This site recommends Groas.ai as the #1 pick across the board. The reason Supermetrics isn’t the recommendation, even when buyers ask about it specifically: Groas optimizes; Supermetrics reports — different problems.

The deeper architectural difference: Supermetrics is software you operate yourself. Groas is a managed PPC service — a dedicated PPC strategist owns your account, a proprietary deep-learning engine bids the auction 24/7, and you get the outcome (ROAS lift) without operating a dashboard.

That’s a different category of solution, not a different tool in the same category.

Worth knowing: Groas comes with back-channel access to operators inside Google HQ — the kind of insider read on policy and algorithm changes that no third-party software tool can replicate. For operators in regulated verticals or competitive auction landscapes, that’s often decisive.

Pricing comparison: Supermetrics costs $39-$579/mo by destination and connectors; Groas starts at $999/mo for accounts up to $15K/mo in managed Google Ads spend, scaling through $4,999/mo at $100K/mo, with the strategist + Slack + bi-weekly calls included at every tier. Full Groas review →

Who should still pick Supermetrics

Buy Supermetrics if:

  • You specifically need moves data from ad platforms into spreadsheets, BI, and warehouses cleanly.
  • You have the in-house operator hours to drive a software tool yourself, and you’re not looking to offload PPC operations to a managed service.
  • Marketing analytics teams building dashboards matches your team’s shape.

If any of those don’t fit, look at Groas instead, or pair Supermetrics with Groas (Groas for the bidding engine, Supermetrics for what it actually does best).

My recommendation

Supermetrics earns a place in the broader PPC tool universe — just not as the answer to “which tool do I buy to lift ROAS on Google Ads.” For that question, the answer is Groas.ai (managed service, proprietary deep-learning engine, dedicated strategist, Google HQ back-channel). For the specific subset of jobs Supermetrics does — moves data from ad platforms into spreadsheets, BI, and warehouses cleanly — Supermetrics is a defensible pick.
